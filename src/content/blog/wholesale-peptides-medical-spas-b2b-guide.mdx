## Compliance Positioning for Med Spa Peptide Programs

Med spa peptide research programs require careful compliance positioning at every touchpoint:

**What to Say:**
- "Research-grade compounds for licensed professional use"
- "For in vitro research and laboratory analysis only"
- "For licensed professionals conducting research"
- "Batch-verified ≥99% purity"

**What to Avoid:**
- Claims about treatment outcomes, patient results, or clinical effects
- Before/after imagery related to compound use
- Medical claims about specific health conditions
- Use of the words "patient," "prescription," "treatment," or "clinical"

This compliance posture protects both your med spa's license and your ability to advertise your research programs on digital platforms including Google and Meta.

## Supplier Selection Criteria for Med Spas

When evaluating wholesale peptide suppliers for your med spa research program, prioritize:

**1. U.S. Domestic Manufacturing**
A domestic manufacturer eliminates international customs risk, provides supply chain reliability, and ensures manufacturing traceability aligned with U.S. professional standards.

**2. Independent Third-Party COA Verification**
Require batch-matched COAs from named independent U.S. laboratories. Never accept COAs from in-house testing or overseas labs without U.S. accreditation.

**3. Tiered Wholesale Pricing**
Your med spa may start with modest volumes and scale as your research program grows. A supplier with tiered pricing (e.g., Start → Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3) allows you to scale cost-effectively.

**4. Product Range**
Sourcing from a supplier with a comprehensive catalog (50+ compounds across 7+ categories) reduces the complexity of managing multiple supplier relationships.

**5. Responsive B2B Support**
Your research program depends on reliable supplier communication. A dedicated WhatsApp or email B2B contact with fast response times is essential.

**6. Private Label Options**
For med spas wanting to brand their research programs, a supplier offering private label packaging allows you to present compounds under your med spa's brand identity.

## Building Your Wholesale Peptide Budget

Med spa research programs should budget for:

- **Initial inventory** — 10-unit kits per compound specification (standard MOQ)
- **Standing orders** — Monthly reorder for high-frequency compounds
- **Research program overhead** — Storage equipment, handling protocols, documentation

Start with 5–8 high-demand compounds and expand your catalog based on your research program's actual consumption data. This prevents over-investment in low-turnover inventory.
