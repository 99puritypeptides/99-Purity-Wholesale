## What Is NAD+?

NAD+ is a coenzyme found in all living cells, playing a central role in metabolic processes including energy production, DNA repair, and cellular signaling. It serves as an electron carrier in redox reactions (converting to NADH) and as a substrate for enzymes including:

- **Sirtuins (SIRTs)** — NAD+-dependent deacylases studied extensively in aging and metabolic research
- **PARP enzymes** — poly(ADP-ribose) polymerases involved in DNA repair mechanisms
- **CD38** — an enzyme implicated in NAD+ metabolism and immune cell function

Research interest in NAD+ has surged following studies demonstrating age-related NAD+ decline in multiple tissue types, leading to substantial investigation into supplementation and precursor compounds (NMN, NR) as research tools for studying aging-related cellular processes.

- Stored in airtight, sealed vials
- Protected from light exposure
- Kept at -20°C for long-term storage
